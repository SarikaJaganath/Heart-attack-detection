OUR PURPOSE

About HeartCare

HeartCare is an educational prototype for reviewing selected cardiovascular factors and demonstrating an appointment request workflow. It is not a medical diagnostic tool.

Medical disclaimer

A normal-looking form result cannot rule out a heart attack. For possible symptoms, seek emergency care immediately. In India, call 112.

Do not enter real patient information into this frontend demo. Real patient data requires a secure backend, access controls, a privacy notice and appropriate review.
